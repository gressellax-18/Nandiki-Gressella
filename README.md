🎯 Problem Statement

Modern warehouses handle multiple operations simultaneously:

Customer orders
Inventory management
Picking and routing
Packing and quality control
Damaged or missing items
Shipment and dispatch tracking
Operational bottlenecks
When these activities are disconnected, warehouse teams may face:

Order processing delays
Inventory mismatches
Picking inefficiencies
Packing and quality issues
Shipment delays
Slow exception resolution
Lack of operational visibility
STOCKPILOT addresses this challenge by bringing these activities into a single intelligent warehouse operations platform.

💡 Our Solution

STOCKPILOT follows a decision-driven warehouse workflow:

Order Received ↓ Inventory Check ↓ Stock Allocation ↓ Picking & Route Planning ↓ Packing & Quality Control ↓ Exception Handling ↓ Shipment & Dispatch ↓ Analytics & Decision History

Instead of simply displaying warehouse data, the platform focuses on:

«Detect → Analyze → Decide → Act → Track»

🧠 What-If Simulator

One of the decision-support features of STOCKPILOT.

The simulator is designed to explore possible operational scenarios before taking action.

Example scenarios:

«What happens if a worker becomes unavailable?»

«What happens if inventory decreases?»

«What happens if order priority changes?»

The purpose is to support better operational planning and decision-making.

🕒 Decision History

Important operational decisions can be tracked for transparency and analysis.

A decision record can contain:

Time ↓ Event / Problem ↓ Recommendation / Action ↓ Decision ↓ Result

This creates an operational history instead of losing important decisions.

💬 Customer Feedback

Provides a dedicated area for capturing customer feedback related to fulfillment and warehouse service.

This can help connect operational performance with customer experience.

🧠 Intelligent Decision Layer

The core concept of STOCKPILOT is a decision-driven workflow.

Example

⚠️ Exception Detected ↓ Analyze Impact ↓ Identify Possible Action ↓ Recommend Decision ↓ Take Action ↓ Track Result

For example, if an order faces an inventory shortage:

Inventory Shortage ↓ Affected Order Identified ↓ Alternative Stock Evaluated ↓ Reallocation Recommended ↓ Order Continues ↓ Decision Recorded

This makes STOCKPILOT more than a simple warehouse management dashboard.
